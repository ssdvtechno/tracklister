Connect Spotify (one time)

Spotify only lets apps make playlists through a free “developer app” you own. It takes about two minutes, and you only do it once.

Create your Spotify app

  1. Click the button below and log in.
  2. Click Create app. Name and description can be anything (e.g. “Tracklister”).
  3. Under Redirect URIs, paste this and click Add:
  1. Tick Web API, agree to the terms, and click Save.

Paste your Client ID

On your new app’s page, click Settings and copy the Client ID. You don’t need the Client secret.

Log in

Your browser opens Spotify’s login page. Approve it and come back here.

Waiting for you to approve in the browser…

Got a 403 or “user not registered” error? In the dashboard, open your app’s Settings → User Management and add the email of your Spotify account.

DJ set → Spotify playlist

Paste a YouTube, SoundCloud or Mixcloud link. Tracklister finds the tracklist (description, chapters or comments) and matches every song on Spotify.
