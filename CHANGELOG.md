# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-11-14

First release.

- Desktop app for Windows and macOS: paste a DJ set link or tracklist, get a Spotify playlist.
- Finds tracklists in YouTube descriptions, chapters and top comments; SoundCloud and Mixcloud via yt-dlp.
- Guided one-time Spotify setup with PKCE login (Client ID only).
- Tracklist cleanup, including splitting "A – X mixed with B – Y" lines into two tracks.
- Review screen with alternative versions, manual search and filters.
- Searches Beatport, Bandcamp, SoundCloud, YouTube, Apple Music and Deezer for tracks Spotify misses, and recovers some of them on Spotify by ISRC.
